## Carbon Footprint

### Training
- **Hardware**: 2x T4
- **Time**: ~8-12 hours
- **Energy**: ~50-100 kWh
- **CO2**: ~25-50 kg

### Inference
- **Per image**: ~0.01 kWh
- **CO2**: ~0.005 kg

### Total
- **Training**: ~25-50 kg CO2
- **Inference**: ~0.005 kg CO2 per image

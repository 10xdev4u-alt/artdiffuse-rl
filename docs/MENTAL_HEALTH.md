# Mental Health and Wellbeing

## Our Commitment

We care about the mental health and wellbeing of our community members.

## Why This Matters

### Community
A healthy community is a productive community.

### Sustainability
Burnout leads to turnover.

### Quality
Happy people do better work.

### Humanity
We are humans first, contributors second.

## Our Approach

### Work-Life Balance
- Respect personal time
- No after-hours expectations
- Encourage breaks
- Support time off

### Sustainable Pace
- Realistic deadlines
- No crunch time
- Quality over speed
- Long-term thinking

### Support
- Mental health resources
- Counseling services
- Peer support
- Professional help

### Recognition
- Celebrate achievements
- Acknowledge struggles
- Show appreciation
- Build each other up

## How to Get Help

### If You're Struggling
1. Reach out to someone you trust
2. Contact a mental health professional
3. Use crisis resources if needed
4. Take time off if necessary

### If You're Concerned About Someone
1. Check in with them
2. Listen without judgment
3. Encourage professional help
4. Support them

# Mission

## Our Mission

To democratize AI art generation by creating small, efficient, and high-quality diffusion models that run on consumer hardware.

## Why This Matters

### The Problem
- Large models require expensive hardware
- Most people can't run state-of-the-art models
- AI art generation is inaccessible to many

### Our Solution
- Small models that run on consumer hardware
- Efficient training and inference
- Open source and free to use

### Our Impact
- More people can create AI art
- Lower barrier to entry
- More innovation and creativity

## How We Do It

# Ethics

## Overview

This document outlines the ethical considerations for ArtDiffuse-RL.

## Responsible AI

### Bias and Fairness

We are committed to reducing bias in our models.

**Our approach**:
- Diverse training data
- Regular bias audits
- Transparent reporting
- Community feedback

### Privacy

We respect user privacy.

**Our approach**:
- No data collection
- Local processing
- Clear privacy policy
- User control

### Safety

We prioritize safety.

**Our approach**:
- Content filtering
- Usage guidelines
- Reporting mechanism
- Continuous monitoring

## Environmental Impact

### Energy Efficiency

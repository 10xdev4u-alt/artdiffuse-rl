# Vision

## Mission

To create a small, efficient, and high-quality diffusion model that makes AI art generation accessible to everyone.

## Goals

### Accessibility
- Run on consumer hardware
- Minimal resource requirements
- Easy to use and deploy

### Quality
- Competitive with larger models
- State-of-the-art results
- Continuous improvement

### Open Source
- Fully open source
- Community driven
- Transparent development

### Innovation
- Novel architectures
- Novel training techniques
- Novel applications

## Values

### Openness
We believe in open source and transparent development. Our code, data, and models are freely available.

### Community
We believe in the power of community. We welcome contributions from everyone.

### Quality
We believe in quality over quantity. We focus on doing things right.

### Innovation
We believe in pushing boundaries. We explore new ideas and techniques.
